Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

1. Zero Server Telemetry

TaskCore is engineered with a strict privacy-first architecture. We do not operate remote tracking servers, third-party user telemetry, fingerprinting scripts, or analytic beacons. Your usage patterns and task details are never transmitted over the internet to our systems or third-party advertising brokers.

2. Client-Side Data Storage

All tasks, subtasks, categories, and user preferences are saved locally inside your web browser using HTML5 LocalStorage. The data resides exclusively on your device storage:

  • No account registration or password is required.
  • Clearing browser storage or site cache will reset stored data.
  • You can export a full JSON backup of all tasks at any time using the Export JSON feature in the sidebar.

3. Cookies and Trackers

TaskCore does not deploy advertising cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking mechanisms. The application operates entirely offline after the initial page load.

4. Data Deletion and Portability

You retain complete ownership and sovereignty over your records. You can delete individual tasks, clear completed items, or import/export backup snapshots directly through the interface without needing external permissions.

5. Policy Updates

Any amendments to this policy will be documented directly within this application view with an updated effective date.
