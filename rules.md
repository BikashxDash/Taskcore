# Project Rules & Constraints (rules.md)

## 1. Core Visual & Aesthetic Rules

### 1.1 No Purple Gradients
- Never use purple-to-blue or magenta gradients (`linear-gradient(135deg, #6366f1, #a855f7)`).
- Use solid, purposeful color fills with neutral backgrounds (slate, zinc, charcoal).
- Allowed accents: Dusty rose (`#d8a2a2`), soft blush (`#ffdcdc`), vanilla butter cream (`#fff9d6`), meadow moss (`#8ea66b`), velvet charcoal (`#181213`), amber warning (`#d97706`).

### 1.2 No Pill-Shaped Buttons
- Button border-radius must remain strictly rectangular with subtle corner rounding (4px to 6px maximum).
- Never use `border-radius: 9999px`, `border-radius: 50px`, or oval shapes for buttons, chips, or inputs.

### 1.3 No Emoji Icons
- Never use unicode emojis (such as , , , , , ️) as icons, button labels, or decoration.
- Use clean, scalable geometric SVG icons with uniform 1.75px stroke width.

### 1.4 No Em Dashes
- Never use the em dash character ("-") anywhere in UI text, code, documentation, or legal pages.
- Use standard hyphens ("-"), colons (":"), or clear sentence structure instead.

### 1.5 No Crazy Scroll Animations
- No scroll hijacking, parallax shifts, 3D rotations, or delayed reveal triggers.
- Scrolling must be native, immediate, and hardware accelerated without lag.

### 1.6 No Cursor Animations
- Use standard operating system mouse cursors (`default`, `pointer`, `text`).
- No trailing particles, trailing circles, cursor followers, or custom magnetic circles.

### 1.7 Custom Favicon
- Supply a dedicated, custom SVG favicon featuring a geometric task check icon.
- Do not use generic browser default or external generic icons.

### 1.8 Remove "Made with AI" Tag
- Never include "Made with AI", "Generated by AI", or any artificial intelligence watermarks in the markup, footer, metadata, or comments.

## 2. Content & Copy Rules

### 2.1 No Fake Reviews or Testimonials
- No fictitious user quotes, fictional customer personas, or fabricated testimonials.

### 2.2 No Fake Metrics or Counters
- Never display fake social proof (such as "Trusted by 100,000+ happy users" or "Over 5 million tasks finished").
- Only display actual, dynamic user task statistics calculated from local data (such as "4 active tasks", "2 completed today").

### 2.3 No Vague Hero Text
- Do not use hollow marketing slogans like "Unlock your potential with next-gen synergy" or "Revolutionize your daily rhythm".
- Use direct, descriptive labels: "Task Manager", "Daily Agenda", "All Tasks", "Add Task".

### 2.4 No AI Slop Copy or Photos
- No stock photos of models staring at laptops or generic 3D abstract clay figures.
- Keep copy functional, direct, and utilitarian. Every button and link must clearly state its exact purpose.

## 3. Architecture & Functional Rules

### 3.1 Mobile Phone and Desktop Support
- Mobile: Touch targets must be at least 44px by 44px. Support bottom navigation, sliding sheets, and one-thumb actions.
- Desktop: Full keyboard navigation (shortcuts for create, search, filter, escape), multi-column layout, and spacious table/card display.

### 3.2 Privacy & Legal Compliance
- Provide a dedicated Privacy Policy view accessible from the navigation.
- Provide a dedicated Terms and Conditions view accessible from the navigation.
- Guarantee 100% local persistence with zero third-party telemetry, zero cookies, and zero external tracking scripts.

### 3.3 Code Quality Rules
- Semantic HTML5 (`<main>`, `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<aside>`, `<section>`, `<dialog>`).
- Pure Vanilla JavaScript without heavy framework baggage.
- Accessible ARIA labels on all interactive controls.
